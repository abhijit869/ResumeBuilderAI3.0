---
name: ResumeGPT private tools
description: Product boundary for no-key resume assistance and offline workspace utilities.
---

ResumeGPT’s secondary assistance tools should default to deterministic, client-side operations over the saved profile and current job analysis. They can draft, score, organize, and improve wording, but must not invent unsupported evidence or imply that a network AI call occurred.

**Why:** Users asked for creative and productive AI-style tools without adding credentials, and resume claims must remain grounded in the user’s own evidence.

**How to apply:** Keep private tools editable and transparent, label them as local/no-key when relevant, and reserve OpenCode Zen for server-side grounded generation that benefits from model reasoning.
